## Selected projects

### B2B Virtual Account Ledger

[View the repository](https://github.com/amityoshops-dev/b2b-virtual-account-engine)

A virtual account management and double-entry ledger platform for transaction banking and treasury operations.

- Implements balanced sub-ledger transactions, virtual account workflows, and ISO 20022 payment processing.
- Built with Python, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL.

### NPCI Payment Rails Simulator

[View the repository](https://github.com/amityoshops-dev/npci-b2b-engine)

A simulator exploring NPCI payment product workflows, including UPI, IMPS, and NACH.

- Models payment initiation, validation, clearing, settlement, and reconciliation.
- Emphasizes traceable transaction states, failure handling, and operational visibility.

### Free AI Router

[View the repository](https://github.com/amityoshops-dev/free-ai-router) (private)

A multi-provider chat app that discovers available models and routes prompts by task.

- Supports automatic, fast, coding, reasoning, and manual model selection.
- Fails over to another model when a provider is unavailable or rate-limited.
